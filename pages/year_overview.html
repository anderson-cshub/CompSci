Upper 6th Curriculum Overview

The Upper 6th curriculum covers the final preparation for OCR A Level Computer Science Paper 1 and Paper 2 whilst supporting the completion of the Non-Exam Assessment (NEA). Topics are delivered across Michaelmas, Lent and Trinity terms.
